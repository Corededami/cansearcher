Help with costs, travel and questions

The study treatment is usually provided at no cost. Ask the trial team what else is covered. These free services can help:

  • National Cancer Institute: 1-800-4-CANCER (1-800-422-6237) free answers about trials, in English and Spanish
  • American Cancer Society: 1-800-227-2345 rides to treatment and free lodging programs
  • Lazarex Cancer Foundation helps pay travel costs for clinical trials
  • Sisters Network Inc. support for Black breast cancer survivors

Trial data comes live from ClinicalTrials.gov, run by the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Match labels are a guide only: the trial team or your doctor decides if a trial is right for you.

We do not save your answers. Trial listings come from ClinicalTrials.gov; your answers go to our AI only to check which trials fit, and are not stored.
